# Case study library

Drop-in case studies for slide 10 (credentials) and anywhere else a
worked example is useful. Each entry is written to fit the
`content_cards` layout (title + three evidence cards) or, for shorter
callouts, the `context` layout.

When picking a case:

- **Match the category.** Food & beverage → YouBrew / CAFEE / Perrier.
  Retail / home goods → Re-feel. Wellness / hydration → Evian. Pro
  appliances / B2B → Girbau. Beverage alcohol → Sparkling wine.
- **Lead with the number.** Every title here includes the headline
  metric; keep it.
- **Paste the block verbatim.** Don't re-write; swap client name only
  if NOBA has asked it to be anonymised.

## RE-FEEL — home goods pack redesign

**Fit for:** retail, category maturity, brand refresh needs.

- **Tag:** Case study
- **Title:** Re-feel — one pack redesign, 18% repeat-purchase lift.
- **Cards:**
  1. **The call** — A category leader, stuck at flat. The pack was
     familiar; familiarity stopped working.
  2. **The work** — Shelf ethnography in four cities, blind A/B of
     two claim architectures, a single redesign.
  3. **The outcome** — 18% repeat-purchase lift in quarter one; ranging
     held at every listed retailer.

## PERRIER ENERGIZE — sparkling water, adjacency test

**Fit for:** brand extension, functional claim, young-adult targeting.

- **Tag:** Case study
- **Title:** Perrier Energize — the extension that didn't dilute the
  parent.
- **Cards:**
  1. **The call** — Extend Perrier into a functional-water space
     without blurring the heritage.
  2. **The work** — Claim test with 900 category buyers, five concept
     routes, shelf trial across three formats.
  3. **The outcome** — One route passed every gate; launched in 11
     markets without parent-brand cannibalisation.

## YOUBREW — youth coffee category

**Fit for:** category creation, Gen-Z, ritual / joy framing.

- **Tag:** Case study
- **Title:** YouBrew — a category made by 23-year-olds, tested by 43-year-olds.
- **Cards:**
  1. **The call** — Make cold-brew matter to a generation that's had
     it since university.
  2. **The work** — Ethnography in student housing, co-creation with
     22 young buyers, claim and pack refresh.
  3. **The outcome** — First-year repeat 34% above category average;
     range locked into three retailers before summer.

## EVIAN — hydration, clinical claim

**Fit for:** wellness, regulated claim space, high stakes.

- **Tag:** Case study
- **Title:** Evian Wellness — the claim that cleared the regulator and
  the shopper.
- **Cards:**
  1. **The call** — Translate a new hydration claim into copy that

## When nothing in the library fits

Ask the user for one of: a prior proposal that worked, a 1-liner about
a recent win, or permission to write a placeholder block flagged as
such. Never fabricate a case — credibility-destroying.
