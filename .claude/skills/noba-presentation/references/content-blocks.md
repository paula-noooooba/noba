# Content blocks — verbatim phrasings

Fixed NOBA copy that recurs across decks. Use these **exactly as
written** — do not paraphrase, shorten, or re-voice. Only the values
bracketed `[like this]` are variables.

When a deck needs one of these blocks, paste the whole thing into the
matching layout rather than re-writing it.

---

## Mission

**Layout:** `dark_outro` — eyebrow + headline.

- **Eyebrow:** Our mission
- **Headline:** Turn uncertainty into the next move. For the brands
  shaping what's next.

## What We Do

**Layout:** `content_cards` — three cards.

- **Tag:** What we do
- **Title:** Strategy, research, and design — under one roof.
- **Cards:**

     flinching. Shelf ethnography, category audits, buyer interviews.

     launch playbooks, sales enablement.

## Methodology phase code-names

Used on timeline, phase-detail, and gantt slides. Default to four
phases; drop to three only when the engagement is under 6 weeks.

| # | Code-name | One-line descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Sense**  | Map the market and the buyer. |
| 02 | **Shape**  | Frame hypotheses and options. |
| 03 | **Stress** | Test with real users. Discard what doesn't hold. |
| 04 | **Ship**   | Package the winning direction, ready to roll. |
