# Voice — before / after pairs

The single biggest lever for deck quality. Run step 5 of the workflow
through this file: for every line of copy in the draft, find the closest
before/after pair and rewrite to match the pattern of the **After**.

Rules applied throughout:

- ≤ 16 words per line. Declarative, no hedging.
- No "leverage", "synergy", "journey", "ecosystem" (generic), "solution",
  "best-in-class", "aim to", "seek to", "strive to", "holistic".
- Concrete nouns over abstract ones. Verbs over nominalisations.
- Outcomes before process. What they walk away with, not what we do.

## Patterns you'll reuse

- **Noun + comma + action**: "Decide, fast, on …"
- **Negation + replacement**: "Not an opinion. Proof." · "Price isn't the
  lever. Story is."
- **Three-beat list**: "Move decisively. Test early. Ship the story."
- **Subject + earns/wins/fails**: "Which SKU earns repeat." · "What
  survives the aisle."

If your rewrite doesn't match one of these four patterns, rewrite again.
