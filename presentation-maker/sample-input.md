Validating Fluidra's Intrapreneur Ventures — Proposal by NOBA

**Expected Impact.** Faster validation of new opportunities. Better investment
decisions for the Innovation Committee. Stronger intrapreneur capabilities
across the participating teams. Capability building so the program can run
future cycles autonomously.

**Context.** Fluidra runs an internal intrapreneurship program. Five teams
present to the Innovation Committee on April 20, covering their opportunity,
hypotheses, validation plan and required resources. Three teams will be
selected and get 2–3 months to validate their ideas. They return to the
Committee with evidence of market potential, a Go/No-Go recommendation and a
roadmap. Winning initiatives feed Fluidra's 2027 investment decisions.

**Objectives.** Focus teams on their most critical assumptions. Design fast,
low-cost experiments. Generate credible market signals. Structure clear
recommendations for the Innovation Committee. In parallel, build the program
manager's hands-on capability to run validation cycles.

**Our Approach.** Teams execute the validation work; NOBA acts as mentor,
challenger, and targeted execution support.

**Phase 1 — Idea & Test Plan Review (early April).** Review of each team's
materials plus a 1-hour working session per team (5 teams) to sharpen critical
hypotheses and validation plans. Outcomes: a clearer hypothesis framework and
a focused validation plan per team. The Fluidra program manager joins all
sessions.

**Phase 2 — Venture Validation Support (end April to end July).** Weekly
mentoring, about 15 hours per team: review experiment results, refine
hypotheses and validation strategy, define next experiments on the path to a
Go/No-Go recommendation. Execution support, up to 30 hours per team: customer
research (interviews, surveys), prototyping and landing pages, digital demand
experiments, business modelling and financial analysis.

**Roadmap Development.** NOBA mentors each team to develop next steps after
the demo day. The roadmap is a deliverable for the July 20 demo day, including
the validation experiments to run if the initiative moves forward.

**Capability Building.** The Fluidra program manager co-runs the whole
process: idea review sessions, mentoring sessions, experiment design,
progressively facilitating sessions themselves, with the goal of running
future validation cycles autonomously.

**Timeline.** Early April: idea and test plan review. April 20: presentations
to the Innovation Committee. End of April to end of July: validation phase.
End of July: final recommendations, feeding September discussions on 2027
investments.

**Team.** One NOBA Program Manager responsible for mentoring, coordination,
tracking progress across projects and mobilising specialists, backed by a
specialist pool covering customer research, design and prototyping, digital
experimentation and growth, and business modelling and venture validation.

**Support per Team (approx. 45 hours each).** Idea review and validation
plan: ~2 hours. Weekly mentoring (~10 weeks): ~15 hours. Execution support:
up to 30 hours. External expenses: ~€1,500 per team.

**Investment.** €13,000 in fees plus €5,000 external costs (€1,000 per team).
Additional support available at €85/hour. Excludes VAT and project expenses.

**Why NOBA.** Customer research and insights. Rapid prototyping and concept
testing. Digital demand experiments. Business modelling and venture
evaluation.
